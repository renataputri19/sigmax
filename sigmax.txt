SIGMA X

Σ X — Satellite Intelligence for Growth & Mapping Analysis

One-liner

SIGMA X reads satellite imagery and real-world activity data across Batam’s key economic zones, then uses Gemma 4 as a reasoning layer to turn raw spatial signals into a ranked, explained growth analysis — which zones are genuinely developing, which are stalling, and why.

Problem

Batam is in active investment-growth mode (KEK/free-trade-zone expansion), but judging real development progress currently relies on manual site visits and fragmented data. There’s no tool that combines satellite evidence of physical construction with ground-truth signals of economic activity (businesses, hotels, footfall) into one explained, continuously-updatable read of “where is Batam actually growing.”

What SIGMA X does

For a set of predefined Batam zones (e.g. Nongsa Digital Park, Batam Centre, Kabil Integrated Industrial Estate, Tanjung Uncang, Sekupang), it:

Computes NDBI (built-up index) and NDVI (vegetation index) change over time from Sentinel-2 imagery, per zone — are structures expanding, is green space shrinking.
Computes POI density and growth per zone from Google Maps data (hotels, F&B, retail, offices) — a proxy for real economic activity, not just construction.
Feeds both signals (plus a satellite crop per zone) to Gemma 4, which acts as the analyst: it cross-reads the quantitative trend against the visual evidence and the POI signal, then produces a ranked, written growth assessment per zone — flagging genuine growth corridors (built-up AND activity both rising), underbuilt-but-in-demand zones (activity rising, construction lagging), and stalled zones (construction rising, no activity following).
Gemma 4’s role — the core differentiator

Gemma is not a caption generator here. It is given structured per-zone stats (ΔNDBI, ΔNDVI, POI growth %) as text plus the actual before/after satellite crop as image input, and has to reconcile them into a judgment call a plain threshold can’t make — e.g. “NDBI rose here but the image shows cleared land, not a finished structure, so treat this as early-stage, not realized growth.” Output: a short written verdict per zone plus a cross-zone ranking. This is the “Best Use of Gemma 4” angle — real multimodal synthesis, not decoration.

Pick 3-4 zones (fewer is fine — depth over breadth for the demo)
